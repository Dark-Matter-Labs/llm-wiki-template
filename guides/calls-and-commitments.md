# Calls and commitments

## Filing a call

1. Give Claude the notes or transcript, and say which wiki it belongs in.
2. Claude saves it as a source, leaving out small talk and anything personal.
3. Claude writes a summary: the decisions, what each person said they hold, and the open questions.
4. Claude adds a **Next steps** table: who, what, stream and state.
5. You say yes before it is saved.

In a team wiki, the call's source stays on the machine that filed it. Only the summary is shared.

## A name means that person said yes

A person's name goes next to an item only when they said yes to it. A meeting tool's suggested owner is not a yes.
A request to someone is not a yes either. Otherwise the item says **Not confirmed**, and the next meeting can ask.

## The stand-up page

In team wikis that have it, a page gathers every open item from the call summaries, grouped by stream. It shows
who holds each item, and never counts items per person or marks anything late.

## Taking an item, or marking it done

Each open item has two links.

1. Press **I'll take it** to put your own name on an item. You cannot put someone else's name on it.
2. Press **Done** when an item is finished.
3. GitHub opens a short, pre-filled form. Press **Create**.
4. About a minute later, the wiki changes that item, rebuilds the page, replies to you and closes the form.

If someone else already holds the item, the wiki tells you to ask them first.

## Closing an item by hand

Open the call's summary, find its Next steps table, and set the item's State to `done`, `dropped` or `moved`.
`dropped` and `moved` are not failures. They mean someone decided.
