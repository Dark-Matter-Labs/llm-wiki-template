# Source proportion: the analysis must not outgrow its source

Read this when a page interprets someone else's document: during `ingest`, and before `publish-check`, `pre-read`,
`publish-web` or `contribute` sends such a page out. Also on "am I over-reading this" or "is this extrapolation".

The failure is not inaccuracy. It is a mapping table longer than the section it maps, a paraphrase where the
source's own words should be, or an inference in the author's voice on a page that goes out under their name.
Indy's correction on 9 August 2026 is the whole of it: **the volume of synthesis relative to the source is the
signal.** When the analysis grows larger than the thing analysed, stop and ask.

## Method

1. **Locate the boundary.** For each block of analysis, name the source section it interprets. A block that cannot
   name its section is new claim-making. It belongs on a page of the wiki's own, not on a page about the source.
2. **Measure.** Compare the length of the analysis with that section. Longer is a signal to stop, not a matter of
   style. The ratio is a heuristic, not a limit. Past 1:1, the block must earn its length out loud.
3. **Check the voice, separately.** Three failures, all seen on one page:
   - **Commentary in the author's document.** Anything the wiki thinks, on a page published under Dark Matter Labs'
     name, which a stranger would read as the author's view. Remove it or attribute it.
   - **Paraphrase in place of a section.** If the source has a §9, the page carries §9, not the wiki's summary of
     it under its heading.
   - **Inference dressed as footing.** Prefer the source's own framing every time it exists.
4. **Mark what survives.** Interpretation that earns its place stays, labelled: *(this wiki's reading:)*,
   "synthesis", or a section of its own. Labelled inference may go out. Unlabelled inference may not.
5. **When in doubt, cut and offer.** Say in one line what you cut and that it can go back. A cut costs one sentence.
   An over-read on a published page costs a correction.

## Output

A clean pass, with the blocks named and their proportions stated. Or the cuts, each with the section it exceeded
and where its content went: removed, relabelled, or moved to a page of the wiki's own. Never a silent trim.

## Rules

- **On a page about a source, the source's own words beat the wiki's better version of them.**
- Length is the signal, not the offence. A long analysis on the wiki's own page is fine. The offence is length that
  borrows the source's authority.
- This does not license thinness. Under-reading is a different failure. `office-hours` and `joker` deal with it.
- It applies hardest to anything leaving the wiki, and to `ingest`, where an over-read gets written down as the
  record.
