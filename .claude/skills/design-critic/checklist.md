# The slop checklist

Fourteen tells and a bonus, from "The vibecoded slop checklist" (credited to @theadnaankhan), shared by Gurden on
3 November 2026. Each tell is a default a model reaches for. The "instead" lines and the notes on our own guides are
this wiki's reading, not the checklist's.

A tell is a default, not a choice. If the guide this wiki follows declares the choice, it is not a tell.

| # | Tell | What it looks like | Instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Too much spacing | Big empty bands between thin sections. | Space that groups things. Tighten until the page reads as one piece. |
| 2 | Generic fonts | Inter or the system font, picked because it was there. | The guide's typefaces, set to a real scale. |
| 3 | Walls of text | A paragraph where a list, a table or a picture would carry it. | One idea per paragraph. Show the shape the content has. |
| 4 | Bland hero | A slogan, a line of filler and two buttons. | Open with the most telling thing from the subject's world. |
| 5 | Gradients everywhere | Colour washes as backgrounds and fills. | Flat colour that means something. Texture from the subject, such as contour lines. |
| 6 | Everything in rounded cards | The page chopped into same-sized boxes with the same radius and shadow. | Group by meaning. Use rules, columns and space before boxes. |
| 7 | Barely any pictures | Icons and shapes where a photo, map or diagram should be. | Real pictures, maps and diagrams of the work. |
| 8 | Purple and blue accents | A violet-to-blue palette that comes from no brand. | The guide's accents, each with a stated meaning. |
| 9 | Italic serif headlines | One word in italic serif for "elegance". | One type voice for headlines, used straight. |
| 10 | Monospace labels | Small tracked capitals in a mono face above every heading. | Labels only where they carry information. |
| 11 | Vague filler copy | "Empowering seamless experiences", "10K+ happy customers". | Specific words and real figures, each with a source. |
| 12 | Massive footers | Columns of links nobody needs. | The few links people use. |
| 13 | Random decorative symbols | Sparkles, plus signs, arrows and asterisks scattered as ornament. | A mark only where it means something. |
| 14 | Pills and hairline borders | Every tag a rounded pill, every box a faint grey line. | Plain words, or one border weight with a reason. |
| Bonus | Random Three.js | A spinning 3D shape that says nothing about the subject. | Motion that answers what a person did, or none. |

## Where our own guides make these choices on purpose

Read on 3 November 2026, after that day's type changes. These are choices, so they are not tells where their guide
applies.

- **d.INO Home** uses mint and blue as meaning colours (8): each says whose a wiki is. Its labels are Ronzino small
  capitals, not a monospace.
- **The xCO style guide** sets everything in Untitled Serif and Untitled Sans, licensed from Klim. The serif is a
  display face used upright (9). Labels are Untitled Sans. DM Mono remains only for code, and in the PNG export,
  which cannot yet load Untitled Sans.
- **The Dm style guide** has lavender and periwinkle accents (8), and the Dm cards use blurred colour fields as
  backgrounds (5).

Outside the place each guide covers, the same pattern is a tell again. If a guide's choice looks like a tell, raise
it with the guide's owner. Never change a guide yourself.
