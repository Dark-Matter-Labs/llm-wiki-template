### Fill-in notes
- **Every contribution type**, not just text — visual designers and coders are
  authors. Name conceptual and condition-building contributors even if they were
  not involved in finalising the output.
- **Departed contributors** stay credited, using the role they held at the time.
- **External frameworks** not originated at Dm: credit the source and link to
  the original; do not present as Dm's own unless substantially transformed.
- **Unpublished internal sources**: 'drawing on internal research by [Full Name],
  [Year]'; if no link, note 'unpublished internal document, [Year]'.
- **Licence**: default CC BY-SA 4.0; CC BY-NC 4.0 to restrict commercial use;
  structured licensing/dedicated vehicle where uptake at speed/scale needs it.
  Unsure (esp. funded-project or partnership work)? Ask #help-publishing.
