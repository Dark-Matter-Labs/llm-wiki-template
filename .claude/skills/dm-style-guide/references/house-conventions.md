# House conventions (Appendix A)

This is the **prescriptive** part of the guide. Its governing principle is
*consistency*, not a single correct answer. The test is not whether a choice
matches this list, but whether **one** choice was made and applied throughout a
piece. A piece that mixes British and American spellings, or toggles between
'Dm' and 'DML', reads as careless however good the argument.

Where the guide is silent: pick a convention, apply it consistently, and — for
house alignment — prefer British English and the plain, short form.

The `scripts/mechanical_check.py` script automates most of the checks below.
It is deterministic and reliable for what it covers. It cannot judge whether a
flagged word is "doing work or padding" — that is the writer's call.

---

## Naming the organisation

| Convention | Rule | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Use 'Dm' consistently | 'Dm' in running text and credit blocks — not DML, DM, or dm. | Pick one and hold it. 'DML' only where disambiguation from another 'Dm' is genuinely needed, and then consistently. |
| Full name on first mention | 'Dark Matter Labs' first, then 'Dm' thereafter. | Not 'Dark Matter Laboratories' or 'DarkMatter Labs'. Apply the same first-mention pattern across the piece. |

## Spelling and mechanics — choose one and apply throughout

- **Spelling**: if a piece is in British English, use British spellings and
  terms consistently (organise, recognise, programme, behaviour, centre). Do
  not mix in -ize / -or / -er American forms. House preference is British
  English.
- **Oxford comma**: use the serial comma as a consistent rule — 'context,
  proposition, and action'. Apply it throughout, not only where ambiguity
  forces it.
- **Dashes**: use a spaced dash — like this — for parenthetical breaks,
  consistently. Do not switch between the spaced hyphen and the closed-up em
  dash (word—word) within a piece.
- **Quotation marks**: single '…' as first level (double inside); smart (curly)
  quotes throughout, never straight (' or ").
- **Numbers**: spell out one to ten; numerals for 11 and above. Always numerals
  with units and percentages (3°C, 40%).
- **Capitalising coined terms**: capitalise a coined term at first definition
  (Smart Commons) and treat it the same way every time after. Do not Capitalise
  Ordinary Concepts for emphasis.
- **Spacing**: one space after a full stop, consistently.

## Words and phrases to use with care

None of these is banned. Each is a flag to check the word is doing specific
work rather than padding. The script surfaces them; a human decides whether each
instance earns its place. Whichever choices are made, apply them consistently.

| Watch for | Why | Prefer |
|---|---|---|
| disrupt, disruptive, disruption | Borrowed Silicon Valley framing; vague and often self-aggrandising. Rarely says what actually changes. | say what is being changed and how — reshape, reconfigure, challenge, replace |
| leverage, utilise | Management filler; usually means a plain verb. | use, draw on |
| unpack, deep dive, double-click | Tired metaphors that signal effort without delivering it. | examine, look closely at |
| transformative, paradigm shift, game-changing | Self-congratulatory; states the conclusion the reader should reach. | describe the actual change concretely |
| holistic, synergy, ecosystem (loosely) | Vague unless specified. 'Ecosystem' is fine when literal or carefully defined. | say what is connected to what |
| empower, enable (vague) | Often hides who does what to whom. | name the actor and the action |
| in order to, the fact that, it should be noted that | Padding. | to; that; (just say it) |
| very, really, quite, arguably | Hedges and intensifiers that weaken prose. | cut, or be specific |
| AI-tells: delve, tapestry, testament to, navigate the complexities, tripled phrases | Read as generic machine prose; the team flags 'too AI' as a failure mode. | rewrite in your own specific words |

This list is not exhaustive. Propose additions on #help-publishing as house
style settles — particularly field-specific terms partners or peers find opaque.
