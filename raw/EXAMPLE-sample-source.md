## Outcomes-based financing for urban forests (fictional briefing note, 2026)

A short note describing a fictional programme, "Greenline", that finances street-tree
planting in three cities — Lisbon, Tallinn, and Cork — by paying for measured outcomes
(canopy cover, stormwater absorbed, heat reduction) rather than for trees planted.

Key points:
- Greenline pays municipalities against verified outcomes, not activity. Payment is
  released only when independent monitoring confirms canopy and cooling targets.
- Lisbon is the lead pilot; Tallinn and Cork joined in a second wave.
- The programme frames citizens as part of the monitoring infrastructure: residents
  report tree health via a simple app, feeding the verification data.
- Early tension: outcome verification is slow and expensive, which delays payments and
  strains municipal cashflow. The note flags this as the main unresolved risk.
