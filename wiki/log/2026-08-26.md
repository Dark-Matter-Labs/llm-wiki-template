# Log — 2026-08-26

## [2026-08-26] repair | The template had no commons path, and the gap failed silently

Creating `heart-power-wiki` from this template surfaced a defect that had been latent since
the two-cut export landed on 2026-08-21.

The template ships the **spoke** export: `tools/export.py` writes `wiki.public.json` only,
and `export.yml` publishes just that to the orphan `export` branch. For a spoke that is
correct — nobody reads a spoke's export. For a **commons** it is wrong in the worst way: a
commons defaults every page to `internal`, so its public cut is empty by construction. The
new commons published a **218-byte graph with zero nodes**, and the workflow went green.
Its two spokes would have synced it nightly, received nothing, and been told it succeeded.

The fix already existed in `xco-team-wiki` and `learning-system-wiki`; there was simply no
route from it to the next commons anybody created. `SETUP.md` now has a step 3b that names
all four commons-only changes — the two-cut export, the private-page advisory, deleting the
Pages deploy, and the scaffolding's own `private` visibility — with a verification command,
because the failure presents as success and cannot be caught by looking at the tick.

**The general shape:** a default correct for the common case becomes a silent defect for the
uncommon one. Found by cloning the export branch and reading it, not by trusting CI.
