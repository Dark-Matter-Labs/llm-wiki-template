# Greenline programme — summary

> Example page. Delete once real sources are ingested.

A briefing note describing **Greenline**, a programme that finances street-tree planting
by paying municipalities for **verified outcomes** — canopy cover, stormwater absorbed,
heat reduction — rather than for trees planted (raw/EXAMPLE-sample-source.md).

## Key takeaways
- Payment is released only when independent monitoring confirms canopy and cooling
  targets — activity alone doesn't trigger payment (raw/EXAMPLE-sample-source.md).
- [[Lisbon]] is the lead pilot; [[Tallinn]] and [[Cork]] joined in a second wave.
- Residents report tree health via an app, making citizens part of the verification
  data pipeline — see [[Citizens as monitoring infrastructure]].
- **Main unresolved risk:** outcome verification is slow and costly, delaying payments
  and straining municipal cashflow (raw/EXAMPLE-sample-source.md).
