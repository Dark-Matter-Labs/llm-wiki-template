# Outcomes-based financing

> Example page. Delete once real sources are ingested.

A model where funders pay against **measured, verified outcomes** rather than activity
or inputs. In [[Greenline programme — briefing note (summary)|Greenline]], municipalities
are paid for canopy cover and cooling actually achieved, not for trees planted
(raw/EXAMPLE-sample-source.md).

## Observed tension
Outcome verification can be slow and expensive. In Greenline this delayed payments and
strained municipal cashflow — flagged as the main unresolved risk
(raw/EXAMPLE-sample-source.md). _[synthesis]_ This suggests a general design question for
this model: who carries the cashflow gap between activity and verified payment?
