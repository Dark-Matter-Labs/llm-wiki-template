# Citizens as monitoring infrastructure

> Example page. Delete once real sources are ingested.

In [[Greenline programme — briefing note (summary)|Greenline]], residents report tree health
through an app, which makes them part of the **verification data pipeline** rather than an
audience for it (raw/EXAMPLE-sample-source.md).

## Observed tension
Payment depends on verified outcomes, and verification depends in part on residents choosing to
report. _[synthesis]_ That makes participation a dependency of the financing model, not a
nice-to-have: a neighbourhood that stops reporting does not merely go unmeasured, it goes unpaid.
Whether that is a strength (local accountability) or a fragility (a funding gap that follows
disengagement) is the open question this page exists to hold.

- [[Lisbon]], [[Tallinn]], [[Cork]] — the cities where it is in use.
