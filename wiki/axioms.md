# Axioms Register

The living register of the **underlying assumptions this work operates from** — the thing
collaborators keep asking to see stated plainly. Deliberately terse: one card per axiom.

Two skills read this page:
- **`joker`** finds the orthodoxy to challenge here, and steelmans the opposite.
- **`reflect`** checks each week whether the work moved an axiom — a change in evidence
  status is the most meaningful thing a week can produce.

## On evidence status

Three values are used, and **`assumptive` is a legitimate, first-class status — not a
weakness to hide.** Most foundational premises in genuinely new work are chosen rather
than proven; pretending otherwise is the dishonest move.

- **evidenced** — supported by external evidence the corpus cites and doesn't re-argue.
- **assumptive** — a chosen premise defining the operating space; coherent, not (yet)
  demonstrated.
- **contested** — the corpus itself holds counter-pressure against it, or it is disputed
  internally.

## How to write one

Copy the card below. Number them `A1`, `A2`, … — numbering is provisional until the
register settles, and renumbering later is fine.

```markdown
### A1 — <short name for the axiom>

**Claim.** One sentence, stated flatly. No hedging — a hedged axiom can't be tested.

**Logic chain.** Because X, and because Y, it follows that Z.

**Depends on.** [[Other Axiom]], [[Some Concept]] — what has to hold for this to hold.

**Evidence status.** `evidenced` | `assumptive` | `contested` — and one line saying why.

**What would change my mind.** The observation that would force a revision. If you can't
name one, the axiom is currently unfalsifiable — say so here rather than leaving it blank.
```
