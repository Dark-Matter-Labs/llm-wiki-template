# Quickstart for the owner

Three things to remember. That's it.

## 1. Add a source
Drag a PDF or document into the `raw/` folder on GitHub, then tell Claude:
> "Ingest the new document in raw and tell me what it says."

Claude reads it, files it into the wiki, and opens a pull request. Merge the PR to save.

## 2. Ask the wiki anything
> "What does the wiki say about [topic]?"
> "Compare [X] and [Y] based on what we've got."

Claude answers from the accumulated knowledge, with sources.

## 3. Keep it healthy
Every so often:
> "Check the wiki — anything stale, missing, or contradictory?"

And when you want a catch-up:
> "Catch me up on what's new."

---

### If you get stuck
Just describe what you're trying to do in plain words. You don't need to know the
folder names or any commands. Claude reads the rulebook (`CLAUDE.md`) every session
and knows how everything fits together.
